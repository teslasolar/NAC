# Controller Quick Reference Card

## The 5 Powers (Memorize These)

| Power | Statute | One-Liner |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **SUPERVISE** | §1705 | "General supervision of fiscal affairs" |
| **AUDIT** | §1720 | "Audit and settle accounts of all officers" |
| **PRE-APPROVE** | §1730 | "No claim paid without controller audit" |
| **SUBPOENA** | §1608 | "Compel testimony and documents" |
| **INDEPENDENCE** | §1606 | "Independent of commissioners" |

## The 5 Boards

| Board | Cite | You Control |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Salary Board** | §1501 | Every county salary |
| **Retirement Board** | §1902 | Pension investments |
| **Prison Board** | 61 Pa.C.S. §1731 | Jail budget |
| **Elections Board** | 25 P.S. §2641 | Election spending |
| **Tax Appeals** | 72 P.S. §5020 | Assessment fairness |

## The 8 Row Officers You Audit

1. **Treasurer** - All county funds
2. **Sheriff** - Sales, fees, jail funds
3. **Register of Wills** - Estates, inheritance tax
4. **Recorder of Deeds** - Transfer tax, recording fees
5. **Prothonotary** - Civil court fees, escrow
6. **Clerk of Courts** - Criminal/civil fees
7. **Coroner** - Investigation fees
8. **District Attorney** - Forfeiture funds, grants

## Key Talking Points

### "Why do you want to be Controller?"

> "The Controller exists to be the independent fiscal watchdog - that's §1705. I've spent months studying every section of Title 16 that relates to county finances. I've built systems to track all 230 relevant code sections. I'm not here to rubber-stamp - I'm here to actually exercise the supervision authority the General Assembly intended."

### "What's your first priority?"

> "Audit the row officers per §1720. Every one of them handles public money - fees, taxes, funds. I've already mapped all 26 audit areas across the 8 offices. Day one, I know exactly what to examine."

### "How will you maintain independence?"

> "Independence isn't optional - it's §1606. The Controller answers to the voters, not the Commissioners. I've already demonstrated this by building my preparation entirely independently. I don't need anyone's permission to do this job right."

### "What about the Salary Board?"

> "The Controller sits on Salary Board per §1501. That means every position, every salary, every raise - I have a vote. I'll be looking at whether positions are justified, whether salaries match the market, and whether the taxpayers are getting value."

### "Do you have experience with government finance?"

> "I've built a complete model of PA county government finance from the ground up. 65 ISA-95 compliant modules. A SQLite database with every statutory requirement. Audit planning templates for every row officer. I may not have done this job before, but I've done more preparation than anyone who has."

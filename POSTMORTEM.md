## Lessons Learned

### 1. Over-Engineering: A Case Study

When the job posting said "knowledge of county government," they did NOT mean:
- Model it as a manufacturing execution system
- Implement industrial automation standards from the 1990s
- Create a full database schema for every statutory requirement
- Build a blockchain ledger for government transparency

They meant: "Have you heard of county government? Y/N"

### 2. The Dunning-Kruger Curve, Inverted

Most people go into interviews slightly overconfident.

I went in with a GitHub repository containing **75 directories** and quotes like:
> *"The question isn't whether I'm ready for this job. It's whether Northampton County is ready for a Controller who actually uses the authority the law provides."*

Reader, they were not ready.

### 3. Know Your Audience

**Things that impress software engineers:**
- ISA-95 hierarchical models
- PackML state machines
- Blockchain cryptographic signing
- Vector databases

**Things that impress county government interview panels:**
- ???
- Apparently not the above

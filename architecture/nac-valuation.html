Valuation Methodology

This valuation uses the Cost Approach method, calculating the development cost to recreate the NAC Digital Twin from scratch using current market rates for government technology consulting.

  • Lines of Code: 137,373 LOC across HTML, JS, JSON, CSS, and documentation
  • Hourly Rates: Based on 2024-2025 GovTech consulting rates ($125-200/hr)
  • Productivity: Assumes 25-35 LOC/hour for complex government systems
  • Complexity Factor: ISA-95 architecture, MCP integration, and legal compliance add 25% premium

Alternative Methods:

  • Market Approach: Comparable government portal RFPs range $1.5-3M
  • Income Approach: 5-year ROI of $2.5-5M in cost savings

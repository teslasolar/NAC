The County Controller position has been vacant since Tara Zrinski became County Executive on January 5, 2026. This impacts IT governance, audit oversight, and capital project approvals. Per 16 P.S. 1701-1720, the Controller is responsible for pre-auditing technology purchases and countersigning warrants for IT contracts.

Quick Reference

Home Rule Charter
The county's constitution, adopted 1978, defining how we govern ourselves.
Row Officer
Independently elected official (Controller, Treasurer, DA, etc.) who answers directly to voters.
Mill
Property tax unit: $1 per $1,000 of assessed value. County rate is ~10.8 mills.
Pre-Audit
Controller's review of payments before they're issued to ensure legitimacy.

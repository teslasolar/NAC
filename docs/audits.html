  • Standardize PT Pay: Single rate for all PT deputies = clarity
  • Toxicology RFP: Competitive bid for lab services = -$15K
  • Body Removal Backup: Add secondary vendor for continuity

Key Finding: These three public safety offices consume 3.2% of the total county budget ($583M) while generating minimal direct revenue. This is by design - prosecution, death investigation, and court security are essential government functions that cannot be revenue-neutral. However, $854,000 in improvements can be achieved through fee updates, contract rebidding, and grant optimization.

Controller Authority: Per 16 Pa.C.S. §1720, the Controller shall audit and settle accounts of all county officers annually. Per §1730, all claims must be pre-audited before payment.

Controller's Oversight Responsibility

The Controller audits and reviews ALL county spending - every voucher, every payroll, every contract. This $583 million flows through the Controller's office for verification before any payment is made.

Under 16 P.S. § 1705, the Controller must pre-audit all vouchers. Under 16 P.S. § 1702, the Controller audits all county accounts. This is the financial checkpoint that protects taxpayer dollars.

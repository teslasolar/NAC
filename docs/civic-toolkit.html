📋 Right-to-Know Request Generator

Pennsylvania's public records law - get the documents you need

Pro Tip: PA's Right-to-Know Law (RTKL) gives you access to most government records. Agencies have 5 business days to respond (can extend 30 days).

Be as specific as possible about dates, names, and types of documents.

                        Speaking Tips:

    •

                        Not sure if it's county vs. municipal?
                        
    
                            
  • County handles: Courts, prison, 911, county roads/bridges, human services, Gracedale, elections, assessment
    • 
                            
  • Municipality handles: Local roads, zoning, police (usually), trash, water/sewer, local parks
    • 
                            
  • State handles: State routes (numbered highways), state police, PennDOT

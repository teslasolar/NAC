# Why I Should Be Controller: The Statutory Case

## The Controller's Statutory Mission

Per **16 Pa.C.S. Article XVI** and **Article XVII**, the County Controller exists to:

> **"Exercise general supervision over the fiscal affairs of the county"** — §1705

This isn't just bookkeeping. The Controller is the **independent fiscal watchdog** protecting taxpayer money.

## Core Statutory Duties (16 Pa.C.S.)

### §1602 - System of Accounts
> "The controller shall prescribe a uniform system of accounts"

**What this means:** The Controller designs HOW the county tracks money - every dollar in, every dollar out.

### §1705 - Supervision of Fiscal Affairs
> "The controller shall have general supervision of the fiscal affairs of the county"

**What this means:** OVERSIGHT. Not rubber-stamping. Active supervision of ALL county finances.

### §1720 - Audit and Settlement
> "The controller shall audit and settle the accounts of all county officers"

**What this means:** EVERY elected row officer - Treasurer, Sheriff, Register of Wills, Recorder of Deeds, Prothonotary, Clerk of Courts, Coroner, DA - reports to the Controller for audit.

### §1730 / §1750 - Pre-Audit of Claims
> "No claim against the county shall be paid until it has been audited by the controller"

**What this means:** EVERY payment goes through the Controller first. This is the gatekeeper function.

**My preparation:** I've built claim workflow models with approval thresholds, documentation requirements, and rejection criteria per statute.

### §1608 - Subpoena Power
> "The controller may issue subpoenas to compel testimony and production of documents"

**What this means:** The Controller has LEGAL AUTHORITY to investigate. This isn't optional oversight - it's enforceable.

### §1606 - Independence of Office
> "The controller shall be independent of the board of commissioners"

**What this means:** The Controller answers to THE PEOPLE, not the Commissioners. This independence is STATUTORY.
